## 5. Statements the numbers no longer support, or support differently
- Timing side channel: the dudect table has FAIL rows and rows that are not measured. P3 is claimed for the
  kernels only (ADR-023); never write "constant time". The kernel-only series of tags and compaction are
  NOT_MEASURED (ISSUE-36), ncu divergence is NOT_MEASURED (ISSUE-18), the SASS reading is manual (ISSUE-19).
- Occupancy: the peak in the macros is the W = 60 layout. Larger windows and populations exceed CAND_K
  (ISSUE-43); the paper must state the sizing limit beside `resMaxOcc`.
- Real-time claim for write_epoch: no number is exported (ISSUE-12). Do not state that a full epoch of 2^16
  slots fits or misses EPOCH_MS: the rerun is ingested and UNSTABLE at that size (unmeasured under valid
  conditions).
- The oracle comparison of M14 (ISSUE-41): do not write that the GPU matches the oracle.
- The `Conv` macros (`resTurnConv`, `resSameConv`, `resTauGConv`, `resTauConvGlobal`, `resTauConvWithin`) are
  exported from the unshuffled baseline of the device run, whose visible round is the 60 s window. The interim
  paper may define that row differently: check the definition before reading a change of `resSameConv` as a
  result. This mapping is a choice of the exporter, recorded here so that the authors can overrule it.
- Figures: results/figures/figures_index.json lists what was copied and what was held back, with the reason.
