# review/ — everything the panel asks to see

| Path | What it is | Built by |
|---|---|---|
| `REVIEW4_CHECKLIST.md` | the rubric scorecard with evidence paths and remaining actions | M21 |
| `SCOPE_COMPLETION.md` | every approved deliverable → where it lives → done / partial / dropped | M21 |
| `DEMO_SCRIPT.md` | the timed demo, who speaks when, and the fallbacks | M21 |
| `QA_BANK.md` | likely panel questions, each answered by the member who built that part | M21 |
| `publication/` | manuscript, venue shortlist, and the **submission proof** (criterion 9) | M19 |

Criterion 9 is the one with no second chance: it needs documentary proof — the confirmation email as a
PDF, the submission or paper ID, and a screenshot of the portal. A finished manuscript that was never
submitted scores zero. Put those three files in `publication/submission_proof/` the day you submit.

## similarity/
The institute's similarity report (Turnitin / Urkund / whatever SCOPE prescribes) plus the exact PDF that
was checked. Draft-review criterion 4 depends on it. Two rules:
- the agent never states a score it did not receive from the tool — no estimates;
- the check runs through your guide or the department and has a queue, so ask a week before the deadline.
A score over the limit is usually self-plagiarism from your own Review-1/2 documents or over-quoted
literature; both are fixed by rewriting in your own words, not by reshuffling sentences.
